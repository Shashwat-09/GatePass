# Privacy Policy — GatePass

_Last updated: [date]_

[Company legal name] ("we") operates the GatePass app and website. This policy explains what we collect and why.

## What we collect
- **Account:** mobile number (for sign-in by one-time code), name, optional email, date of birth and gender.
- **Bookings:** events, tickets, amounts, payment reference ids, and gate check-in records.
- **Device & diagnostics:** crash reports and anonymous usage events (Firebase Crashlytics/Analytics). We do not use the advertising ID.
- **Payments** are processed by Razorpay. We never receive or store your card, UPI PIN or bank credentials.

## Why we use it
To create and secure your account, sell and deliver tickets, issue tax invoices, admit you at the venue, prevent fraud, provide support, and improve app stability.

## Who we share it with
Only service providers acting for us: SMS delivery ([MSG91/Twilio]), payments (Razorpay), hosting ([provider]), crash/analytics (Google Firebase), and event organisers (your name and ticket details for entry). We do not sell personal data.

## Retention
Account data is kept while your account is active. When you delete your account we immediately erase your name, phone number, email, date of birth and gender. Booking and invoice records are retained without contact details for the period required by tax law.

## Your rights
You can view and edit your profile in the app, and delete your account at **Profile → Delete account**. For any other request (access, correction, grievance) contact [grievance officer name], [email].

## Security
Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens are stored in your device's secure keystore.

## Children
GatePass is not for children under 13.
